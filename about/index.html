About / Media

The Outlet

PxP Sports Media is an independent digital sports-media outlet based in Hickory, North Carolina, covering the storylines fans are actually talking about — verified storylines, data-driven analysis, and real sports culture across the NFL, NBA, college football and basketball, and the wider sports calendar.

How PxP Publishes

Daily. A long-form recap show on game mornings, player story pieces, debate and analysis shorts, and daily coverage posts — published consistently across four platforms, plus the membership home on Patreon.

Publisher / on-camera reporter: Hakeem McCall — founder, publisher, and the outlet's interviewer and host.

PxP Sports Media LLC — formation in progress, North Carolina (Nov 2026). Assignment letters are issued per event by the publisher.

What PxP Brings to Your Program

  • Respectful, professional coverage — verified facts, credited footage under fair-use commentary norms, no gotcha editing.
  • Speed — same-day and next-morning publication while the storyline is still alive.
  • Social-native formats — vertical clips and story pieces built for the platforms recruits and fans live on.
  • A growing North Carolina audience — NC schools, NC athletes, and NC storylines get priority placement.
  • Accountability — PxP grades its own analysis in public, on the record, every morning.
