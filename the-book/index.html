How Grading Works

  • The pair is posted before the games — the line and the board are named.
  • Results enter only from verified official finals — never live numbers.
  • Every leg keeps the grade it was posted with (CERTIFIED / STRONG LEAN / LEAN).
  • PASS and dark nights count as nothing — discipline is not a loss.
  • The weekly rollup posts every Monday; the monthly close on the 1st.

Why We Publish It

Anybody can post a pick. The product here is the record: two free picks every day, graded out loud the next morning whether they cashed or died by one catch. That's the deal, and it never changes.
