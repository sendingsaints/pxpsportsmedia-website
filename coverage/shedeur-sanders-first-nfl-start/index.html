PXP RECAP — EPISODE 3

Shedeur Sanders' First NFL Start: 297 Yards, 2 TDs — and an OT Heartbreak

Cleveland's rookie went 19 of 31 for 297 yards and two touchdowns in New Orleans — then the Saints closed it 23–22 in overtime. The Recap grades the debut, the J.J. McCarthy scare, and our own free pair. Receipts included.

November 9, 2026•Long-form Recap•PxP Sports Media

First NFL start. Fourth quarter, on the road. Shedeur Sanders went deep — 64 yards to KC Concepcion for the lead — and almost stole the whole day. The final line from New Orleans: 19 of 31, 297 passing yards, two touchdowns, zero interceptions, a 114.6 passer rating, plus 28 rushing yards — 325 total yards in start number one. No panic, no picks: a rookie playing like the moment was his.

Debuts grade on the scoreboard, though, and the ending got away from Cleveland. New Orleans answered the late touchdown, a 51-yard field goal forced overtime at 16–16, and in the extra period Tyler Shough found Juwan Johnson to put the Saints up seven. Cleveland drove it right back, scored, and went for two — and the win — instead of the tie. They didn't get it. Saints 23, Browns 22, in overtime.

Story two was the scare in Philadelphia. Giants quarterback J.J. McCarthy took a hit on his hip in the third quarter and walked off. Jameis Winston stepped in for exactly one series — 6 of 8 for 71 yards and the go-ahead touchdown to Isaiah Likely — then McCarthy returned for the fourth, finished 12 of 19 for 127, and the Giants closed out a 19–11 road win. Down, back, done.
