Toppin Missed by One (9), Two Tickets Cashed — Nov 13 Card Graded

The free pair died by one point — Obi Toppin needed 10 and got 9 — but two tickets cashed anyway: THE FIVE's last pair standing and the rooms' hockey pair went 2–0. The receipts, graded in public.

November 14, 2026•Long-form Recap•PxP Sports Media

One point. That was the whole margin. Obi Toppin needed 10 against an OVER 9.5 line — he finished with 9 in 16 minutes of the Pacers' 106–104 win over the Knicks, and he never played the closing stretch. The desk grades the process miss out loud: a moved number, and a role read that didn't survive the game script.

The other leg did its job. Mitch Marner assisted Rasmus Andersson's second-period goal — the go-ahead goal — and his OVER 0.5 assists cashed. Marner's leg also carried THE FIVE's last pair standing: with Davion Mitchell's 7 field goals (22 points, 17 assists in Miami's 133–121 win over Cleveland), pair #5 cashed. And in the rooms, Marner plus Brock Boeser went 2–0 — Boeser's assist on Pettersson's goal in the Rangers' 5–1 win over Vancouver. Boeser's line was a Goblin — reduced payout, labeled plainly.
