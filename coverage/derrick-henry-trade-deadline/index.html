Derrick Henry Trade Deadline Watch: Will the Ravens Move the King by 4 PM?

Baltimore has lost four straight, both quarterbacks are on injured reserve, and the NFL trade deadline hits Tuesday at 4 PM ET. The case for a Derrick Henry trade, the case against it — and the receipt that says this desk read Henry right.

November 10, 2026•Short — News Analysis•PxP Sports Media

The trade deadline hits Tuesday, November 10, 2026 at 4:00 PM ET — and the biggest name on the board is Derrick Henry. Baltimore has lost four straight to fall to 4–5, the latest a 33–10 loss to Jacksonville on Thursday night. Lamar Jackson (thumb surgery) and Tyler Huntley (turf toe) both went on injured reserve on November 5. At 4–5, the season is slipping — and a healthy asset on a broken team is exactly what deadline week feeds on.

The contract math is why the league is calling. Henry's 2026 base salary is $1.255M — cheap for a contender to absorb — while 2027 carries $12M with $0 guaranteed, and he turns 33 on January 4. ESPN's Dan Graziano listed Henry as a conditional “name to watch,” A to Z Sports reported two teams with serious interest, and Green Bay has been floated as a fit (that last part is speculation, and the piece labels it as such).

The case against a move is Henry himself. On Thursday night he passed Bettis and Tomlinson to reach 7th all-time with 13,707 career rushing yards, and he said on the record he's not going anywhere: “I have not mentioned me going anywhere else… Let's go back to work.”

And there's a receipt. Before that Thursday game, PxP's APEX desk graded Henry's rushing line — 68.5 yards on PrizePicks — a PASS and stayed off it. He finished with 57. The desk read Henry right; this piece is the deadline-day verdict on whether Baltimore picks up the phone.
