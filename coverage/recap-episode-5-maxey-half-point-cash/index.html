Tyrese Maxey 24 Points Cashed Our Pick by Half a Point — Ohio Blew a 17–0 Lead

24 points. The line was 23.5. Maxey cashed the free play by half a point — and the other leg watched a 17–0 lead turn into a 34–17 loss. The Recap grades both, out loud, with the receipts.

November 11, 2026•Long-form Recap•PxP Sports Media

Half a point. That was the whole margin. Tyrese Maxey needed 24 to cash our free play at OVER 23.5 points — he scored exactly 24 in Boston's 123–101 win in Philadelphia, and the Strong Lean we posted before tip came home by the thinnest line on the board.

The other leg was the opposite movie. Ohio led Miami (OH) 17–0 — then gave up 34 straight and lost 17–34. A blown three-score lead is the MISS, graded on the same table as the win, like always.
